# Job Hunt OS — Trademark and Branding Policy (Project Policy)

This policy addresses the project's names, logos, domain names and visual identity (the "Project Marks") owned or legitimately controlled by Rohan Roy. It does not assert registration where none exists.

## Generally acceptable references

- Truthfully identify software as derived from or compatible with Job Hunt OS.
- Refer to the project in articles, security research and independent documentation.
- Preserve factual copyright, license and attribution notices.

## Permission required

- Marketing a commercial product, hosted service, domain or application as "Job Hunt OS" or confusingly similar to the official offering.
- Using project logos or branding on a commercial service, merchandise, advertising or endorsement materials.
- Claiming official affiliation, partnership, support, certification or endorsement without authorization.

Noncommercial forks should use distinctive branding and make their unofficial status clear. A separate commercial software license does not automatically confer trademark rights unless expressly included.

For branding permission requests, use the [Commercial Licensing inquiry form](https://github.com/triump0870/AI-Job-Hunt/issues/new?template=commercial-license-request.md) without revealing confidential material.

This document is a project naming policy, not a claim to rights beyond those existing under applicable trademark law. Have counsel review before making enforcement claims.
